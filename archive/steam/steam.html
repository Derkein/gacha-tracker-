What the number is

How many people are in the game on Steam right now — Valve's own live count, read straight from the public Steam Web API. Everything else on this site is a third-party estimate of money; this is the one figure that is actually measured, and published by the platform itself. It is not revenue and doesn't imply any.

What it isn't

  • Not the whole player base. Steam counts only players who launched through Steam. The same game's mobile, PlayStation and standalone-launcher players are invisible to it, so the figure is a floor — and the gap between two games reflects how PC-heavy each one is at least as much as how big it is.
  • Not a daily peak. One reading is kept per day, taken at roughly the same wall-clock time each day (~00:30 JST). That's a fixed-time slice — comparable day to day, which is what a trend needs, but lower than the day's high.
  • Not backfilled. Valve publishes the current count and nothing else, so the history starts the day this scrape started and grows one point a day. There's no archive to import.

Which games have it

On Steam and tracked here: Zenless Zone Zero, Wuthering Waves, Umamusume: Pretty Derby and Neverness to Everness. Arknights: Endfield has a Steam page but its build is still listed as coming soon, so it will start reporting on its own the day it launches there.

Genshin Impact and Honkai: Star Rail are not on Steam and won't be — HoYoverse ships them through its own launcher and the Epic Games Store. Their tile is simply absent rather than empty; there is nothing to show.
