How the order is decided

About these numbers. Japan figures are game-i's revenue estimates (App Store + Google Play Japan, in yen). China figures are Qimai's model estimates for China's iPhone App Store (US$). Both are third-party estimates, not official figures, and they measure different stores, so they are never added together. A banner's month figure is its share of that month; peak ranks are the best daily top-grossing position it reached in that month. Months before a game's game-i monthly series use the sum of its banner figures. Full methods on the tracker.
